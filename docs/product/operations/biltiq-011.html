BILTIQ-011 — Operations Runbook

Generated on the inline fallback path (vLLM unreachable). Prose fidelity is reduced; review before publishing.

BILTIQ-011 adds the regime attestation seam (Regime ABC + frozen ComplianceCheck/ComplianceReport dataclasses) and DPDPRegime, porting CDSCO's eight DPDP/NDHM/ICMR compliance checks. validate(original, anonymised, detections, audit_records, *, generated_at) returns a typed report; compliant requires all eight checks to pass. Residual scan reuses the BILTIQ-002 Indian patterns plus a ported EMAIL pattern. Configurable: generalisation_markers (drives DPDP-1 filtering and DPDP-4 detection; ('XXXX',) restores source-strict) and include_values (False default keeps PII out of report evidence). Caller-supplied timestamp; no clock/env/network; import-light.
